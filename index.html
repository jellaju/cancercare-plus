FROM RESEARCH TO A WEB COMPANION

Built around your experience.

The research foundation

This web adaptation draws on Julaluck Tantipaswasin’s Faculty of Medical Technology, Mahidol University project, Designing a Mobile Application for Breakthrough Cancer Pain Assessment and Personalized Pain Relief (academic year 2566), and its accompanying presentation.

The project’s three pillars—assessment, personalized self-care suggestions, and visual summaries—shape this app. This is a new web implementation, not the original FlutterFlow app or a clinically validated replica.

How personalization works

A latest record with “quite a bit” or “very much” anxiety highlights quiet breathing, inspired by the project’s anxiety-to-meditation example. Otherwise, your saved preference determines the featured activity. All activities remain available. This simple rule is transparent, not a clinical decision algorithm.

What is different from the research prototype?

This version stores data only in your browser. It has no account authentication, Firebase database, provider portal, cloud sync, push alerts, or medical monitoring. No source PDFs or research screenshots are published with the code.

Your data

Records are unencrypted in this browser’s local storage. Anyone using this browser profile may see them. Clearing site data removes them. CSV downloads and printed summaries contain health information; keep them private. External education links open another service.
